Rewards are what shoppers spend their points on. Create a few at different point levels so shoppers always have something to aim for.

## Before you start

- You need access to the **Loyalty** area of the Admin Portal.
- **Choose the reward type:**
  - **Order total**: a $ or % discount on the whole order.
  - **Product**: a discount on a product you choose.
- **Decide the points it costs.** Compare it with your earning rate. See [Set up your loyalty program](../set-up-your-loyalty-program/).
- **Prepare an image** for the reward card.
- **If you redeem rewards in store through your POS**, create a matching discount in your POS first. Give it the same name as the reward and the same discount type. Ask the Mosaic support team if you're not sure.

## Steps

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Loyalty** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Rewards** tab, then select **Create New**. A panel opens on the right.
3. Enter the **Name** and **Description**. Shoppers see these.
4. Upload the **Image**.
5. Choose the **Reward Type**, then complete its settings:
   - **Order total**:
     1. In **Type of redeemable discount**, choose $ or %, and enter the discount.
     2. Enter the **Threshold Value**: the minimum order total for the reward to apply.
     3. Enter the **Point Valuations**: how many points the reward costs.
   - **Product**:
     1. Use **Product Search** to find and select the product.
     2. Enter the **Minimum Cart Total** the order must reach.
     3. Enter the **Point Valuations**.
6. If a **POS Discount ID** field appears, enter the ID of the matching discount in your POS.
7. Select **Create Reward**.

:::caution
For $ rewards, set a **Threshold Value** higher than the discount. This stops a reward from making an order free.
:::

## What to expect

- The reward appears in the **Rewards** list.
- Signed-in shoppers with enough points see it on the **Loyalty** page and in the cart under **Apply Offers & Rewards**.
- To change or remove a reward later, open the menu on its row in the **Rewards** list.

## Examples

**Starter reward.** Example Dispensary creates "$5 off your order": Order total, $5, **Threshold Value** $25, 200 points.

**Bigger reward.** "$10 off your order": Order total, $10, **Threshold Value** $50, 400 points.

**Product reward.** "Free pre-roll": Product, the house pre-roll, **Minimum Cart Total** $40, 600 points.

With rewards at 200, 400 and 600 points, a shopper earning 1 point per $1 reaches their first reward after spending $200.
