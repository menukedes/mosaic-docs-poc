Check how a shopper earned and spent their points, and correct their balance when needed, for example when an in-store purchase wasn't attached to their account.

## Before you start

- You need access to the **Customers** area of the Admin Portal.
- Have the reason for the change ready, such as the date and amount of a missed purchase. You'll record it with the change.

## Steps

### Check a shopper's points history

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Customers** from the left menu.
2. Find the shopper.
3. Open the menu on their row and select **Points History**.
4. Select **View More** on an entry to see its details.

### Add or remove points

1. In **Customers**, find the shopper.
2. Open the menu on their row and select **View/Edit Points**.
3. Choose **Add** or **Remove**.
4. Enter the number of points.
5. In **Description**, enter the reason, for example "In-store purchase on October 12 not attached to account".
6. Select **Save**.

:::caution
Check the points history before you add points, so you don't credit the same purchase twice.
:::

## What to expect

- The shopper's balance updates, and the change appears in their points history with your description.
- To review all points changes for your store, download the **Points Audit Log** from **Customers**.

## Examples

**Missed in-store points.** A shopper bought $60 of products in store, but the budtender didn't attach their account. Example Dispensary earns 1 point per $1, so a manager adds 60 points with the description "In-store purchase on October 12, $60, not attached".

**Goodwill.** A shopper's order was delayed. The manager adds 50 points with the description "Goodwill: delayed order".
