## A shopper didn't get points for an in-store purchase

1. **Wait a day.** In-store points can take up to a day to appear.
2. **Check the sale had the right customer.** Points only go to the customer attached to the sale in your POS. Look shoppers up by phone number, then email.
3. **Check for duplicate customers in your POS.** If the shopper has two records, merge them in your POS.
4. **Check when they signed up.** Purchases made before they created their account don't earn points.
5. If points are still missing, add them yourself. See [Manage a shopper's points](../manage-shoppers-points/).

## A shopper didn't get points for an online order

Online points are added when the order is completed, not when it's placed. Until then they show as pending. Canceled orders don't earn points.

## A shopper earned fewer points than expected

Points are earned on the subtotal after discounts, so offers and rewards reduce the points earned. Also check the shopper's loyalty tier and its **Points per currency**.

## A shopper can't see rewards on the storefront

- They need to be signed in. Guests don't see points or rewards.
- They need enough points for the reward, and their cart must meet its **Threshold Value** or **Minimum Cart Total**.

## A reward disappears at the payment step (in-store POS rewards)

The reward in Mosaic and the discount in your POS must match. Check that they have the same name and the same discount type, for example an order-level discount for an order total reward. Contact the Mosaic support team if they still don't match.

## A reward can't be applied in store

Check the shopper's customer record in your POS has an email address. Add it if it's missing, then try again.

## A shopper was charged points twice for one reward

This happens if the same reward is submitted more than once. Check the shopper's points history, then add the extra points back with a clear description. See [Manage a shopper's points](../manage-shoppers-points/).

## A shopper moved up a tier but doesn't see their new tier's offers

Ask them to sign out of the storefront and sign back in.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can shoppers use more than one reward in an order?**
No. Shoppers can use one reward per order.

**Can shoppers use a reward and an offer together?**
Yes. A reward can be used with a product or category offer in the same order.

**Do points expire?**
Contact the Mosaic support team to check your program's point expiry settings.

**Can I give shoppers points for signing up?**
Yes. Ask the Mosaic support team to set it up.

**What happens to points when an order is returned?**
For a full return or void, the points earned are taken back and any points spent on a reward are returned. For a partial return, the points earned are recalculated.

**Is this the same as Mosaic Pay & Rewards?**
No. Your loyalty program is your store's own. Mosaic Pay & Rewards has a separate points program run by Mosaic.
