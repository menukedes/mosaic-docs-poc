Set how many points shoppers earn and, if you want them, the loyalty tiers your best shoppers can reach. The Admin Portal calls loyalty tiers **Levels**.

## Before you start

- You need access to the **Loyalty** area of the Admin Portal.
- **Decide your structure.** A single tier, where everyone earns at the same rate, is the simplest. Two or three tiers reward your regulars with faster earning.
- **Decide your earning rate.** For example, 1 point for every $1 spent. Think about it together with your rewards: if a $10 reward costs 400 points, a shopper earning 1 point per $1 gets $10 back after spending $400.
- **Moving from another loyalty program?** Ask the Mosaic support team to bring your shoppers' existing points across before you launch.

:::tip
When you join Mosaic, the Mosaic team sets up your program with you and trains you to manage it yourself. Use this page to make changes later.
:::

## Steps

### Set up your first tier

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Loyalty** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Levels** tab.
3. Edit the first level.
4. Enter the **Level Name**, for example "Member".
5. In **Points**, enter the points a shopper needs to reach this tier. For your first tier, this is usually 0, so every new shopper starts here.
6. Choose the **Currency** and enter the **Points per currency**: how many points shoppers in this tier earn for each dollar. Decimals are allowed: 0.5 means 1 point for every $2.
7. Make sure **Enable** is on.
8. Select **Save**.

### Add more tiers (optional)

9. Edit the second level and turn on **Enable**.
10. Enter its **Level Name**, the **Points** needed to reach it, and its **Points per currency**. Use more points and a higher earning rate than the tier below.
11. Choose whether **Downgrade** is on, which lets shoppers move back down from this tier.
12. Select **Save**.
13. Repeat for the third level if you want one. Turn on tiers in order: the second before the third.

:::note
Some settings may not be available for you to change. If you need a change you can't make, contact the Mosaic support team.
:::

## What to expect

- Shoppers move up to a tier as soon as their points reach it.
- You can now limit offers to certain tiers. See [Create an offer](../../marketing/offers/create-an-offer/).

## Examples

**Single tier.** Example Dispensary keeps it simple: one level called "Member", 0 points to join, 1 point per $1. Rewards start at 200 points.

**Three tiers.**

| Level Name | Points | Points per currency |
|---|---|---|
| Member | 0 | 1 |
| Silver | 500 | 1.25 |
| Gold | 1,500 | 1.5 |

A Gold shopper spending $100 earns 150 points, while a Member earns 100. Example Dispensary also runs a monthly Gold-only offer.
