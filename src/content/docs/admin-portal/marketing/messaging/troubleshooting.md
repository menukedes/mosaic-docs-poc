## I can't send: the audience has 0 recipients

The message shows "The selected target audience has 0 recipient(s)" when nobody matches your selection. Recipients start empty, so choose all registered shoppers or at least one segment. If you also chose loyalty tiers, check that some shoppers in your segments belong to those tiers.

## The send button isn't available

Tick the confirmation box that says the campaign is ready to be sent out. The send button only becomes available after that.

## My message has been In Progress for a long time

Large audiences are sent in batches, so big campaigns take longer. Select **Refresh** on the **Messaging** list to update the status.

## My message failed

The reason is shown with the message in the list. A common cause is a very large email, for example one with many large images. Use compressed images, then clone the message and send it again.

## Some shoppers didn't get the email

The recipient breakdown explains who was skipped:

- **Unsubscribed**: shoppers who opted out using the unsubscribe link.
- **Blacklisted**: addresses that can't receive email, for example because earlier emails bounced.
- **Duplicates**: the same address appearing more than once is sent one copy.

## Some shoppers didn't get the push notification

Push notifications only reach shoppers who have allowed notifications. Shoppers who reinstall the storefront app on their phone need to allow notifications again.

## The email looks different in Outlook

Some email apps, especially Outlook on desktop, display images and banners differently. Always send an admin preview and check it in the email apps your shoppers commonly use before sending.

## Statistics aren't showing

Statistics appear once a message has finished sending. If the status is still **In Progress**, wait and select **Refresh**. Statistics for campaigns older than a few weeks may not be available.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I send text messages (SMS)?**
No. Mosaic Messaging sends emails and push notifications only.

**Can I send to a list of email addresses I upload?**
No. Messages go to your registered shoppers, chosen as all registered shoppers, segments or loyalty tiers.

**What time zone do scheduled messages use?**
Your store's time zone.

**Can shoppers unsubscribe?**
Yes. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribed shoppers are skipped automatically.

**Can I change a message after it's sent?**
No. Clone it, make your changes and send it again.
