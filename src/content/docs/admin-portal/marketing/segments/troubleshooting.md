## A shopper isn't in the segment I expected

Segments are based on purchases over the last six months, and on profile details for **Senior** and **Veteran**. Check:

- **New Users** only includes shoppers who registered in the last 30 days.
- **Win Back** only includes shoppers registered for at least six months with no purchase in the last six months.
- **Senior** depends on the date of birth in the shopper's profile.
- **Veteran** depends on the shopper confirming it in their profile.
- Guests without an account aren't in any segment.

## The Segments page or recipient list is slow to load

Stores with many shoppers can take a little longer to load segment counts. Wait a moment and reload the page. If it still doesn't load, contact the Mosaic support team.

## A shopper who recently became a Senior or Veteran didn't get an offer email

Offer emails go out when the offer starts, to the shoppers in the segment at that time. To reach shoppers who joined the segment later, send them a message from **Messaging** to that segment.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I create my own segments?**
Not in the Admin Portal. Contact the Mosaic support team if you need a different segment.

**Can I change the rules for a segment, such as the senior age?**
The senior age is set for your store by Mosaic. Contact the Mosaic support team to change it.

**Will a shopper in two selected segments get my message twice?**
No. Each shopper receives it once.

**Do segments include guest shoppers?**
No. Only registered shoppers are placed in segments.
