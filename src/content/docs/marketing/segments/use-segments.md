You choose segments while creating a message or a Date and Time offer. This page shows where to pick them and what happens when you combine them.

## Before you start

- Read the [segment definitions](../) and decide who you want to reach.
- For messages, decide whether to also narrow by loyalty tier.

## Target a message

1. Go to **Marketing** → **Messaging** and select **Create New**.
2. Under **Recipients**, choose either all registered shoppers or one or more segments.
3. *(Optional)* Choose one or more loyalty tiers to narrow the audience further.
4. Check the recipient breakdown before sending.

For the full steps, see [Create and send a message](../../messaging/create-a-message/).

## Target a Date and Time offer

1. Go to **Marketing** → **Offers** and select **Create New**.
2. Choose **Registered Users** as the audience.
3. Choose the Date and Time offer type.
4. In **Who will receive this offer**, select the segment.
5. Complete the rest of the offer and save it.

For the full steps, see [Create an offer](../../offers/create-an-offer/).

## What to expect

- **Recipients start empty.** In Messaging, nothing is pre-selected. Choose your segments every time, including when you reuse an older message.
- **Combining segments** reaches everyone in any of the selected segments, once each.
- **Adding loyalty tiers** limits the message to shoppers in the selected tiers within the selected segments.
- **Choosing all registered shoppers** sends to every segment and every loyalty tier.
- **Push notifications** go only to shoppers in the selected segments or tiers who have allowed notifications.

## Examples

| Goal | Channel | Segments | Loyalty tiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome new shoppers with a first-visit tip | Email | New Users | *(none)* |
| Thank your most loyal shoppers | Email and push | OGs | Your top tier |
| Bring back lapsed shoppers with an offer | Date and Time offer | Win Back | n/a |
| Veterans Day appreciation | Email | Veteran | *(none)* |

**Win back lapsed shoppers.** Example Dispensary creates a Date and Time offer for **Win Back** shoppers: 15% off, valid for two weeks. They then send an email to the **Win Back** segment announcing it.
