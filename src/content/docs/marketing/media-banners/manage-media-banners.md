After you create media banners, you manage them from the **Media Banners** list: switch them on and off, update them, or remove them.

## Find your banners

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Marketing** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Media Banners** tab.

The list shows each banner with its title and status (**Active** or **Inactive**). Each row has a **⋯** menu with the actions below.

## Activate or deactivate a banner

1. Find the banner in the list.
2. Open its **⋯** menu.
3. Select **Activate** to let the banner appear on the storefront, or **Deactivate** to hide it.

The menu always offers the opposite of the banner's current status: an active banner shows **Deactivate** and an inactive banner shows **Activate**. You can also show or hide a banner with its checkbox in the [reorder panel](../arrange-your-landing-page/).

:::tip
Deactivate rather than delete banners you might use again, such as a recurring holiday notice. Inactive banners stay in your list but are never shown to shoppers.
:::

## Edit a banner

1. Open the banner's **⋯** menu and select **Edit**.
2. Change what you need, such as the title, images or video, links or schedule.
3. Select **Save & Close**.

The store a banner belongs to can't be changed after the banner is created. To show the banner at a different store, create a new banner for that store.

## Delete a banner

1. Open the banner's **⋯** menu and select **Delete**.
2. Confirm.

:::caution
Deleting a banner removes it from your list and from the landing page. Deactivate it instead if you might need it again.
:::

## What to expect

- Changes appear on the storefront landing page the next time it loads. Refresh the page to check.
- A deactivated or deleted banner disappears from the landing page.
- A scheduled banner that you activate still appears only within its schedule.
