## My banner isn't showing on the storefront

Check these in order:

1. **Is it active?** In **Marketing** → **Media Banners**, check the banner's status. If it's **Inactive**, open its **⋯** menu and select **Activate**.
2. **Is it ticked in the reorder panel?** Open the reorder panel and make sure the box next to the banner is ticked. See [Arrange your landing page](../arrange-your-landing-page/).
3. **Is it within its schedule?** If the banner is scheduled, check the start and end dates, the selected days and the time window. Remember that schedules follow your store's time zone.
4. **Is it for the right store?** Each banner belongs to one store. Check you're looking at that store's storefront.
5. **Refresh the storefront.** Changes appear the next time the landing page loads.

## My banner looks cropped

Banners fill the width of the screen. Images and videos that aren't wide and tall enough for a banner can have their sides cropped. Use a landscape design made for a banner, keep important text and logos away from the edges, and check it on both a phone and a computer.

## My image or video won't upload

Rename the file using only letters, numbers and dashes, such as `holiday-hours.jpg`, then upload it again. Apostrophes and other special characters in file names can stop an upload. If it still fails, try a smaller version of the file.

## I can't save my scheduled banner

If **Save & Close** stays unavailable, check the **Visible Time Window**: the **End Time** must be later than the **Start Time**. Also check that the **End Date**, if set, is after the **Start Date**.

## My banner stopped showing by itself

If the banner had an **End Date**, it stops appearing after that date and its status changes to **Inactive**. To run it again, edit the banner and choose new dates.

## Shoppers can't click my banner

- A banner is only clickable if you added a link to it. Edit the banner to add one.
- On in-store kiosks, banners are never clickable, so shoppers stay in your store.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can one banner appear at several stores?**
No. Each banner belongs to one store. Create a separate banner for each store.

**Can I change which store a banner is for?**
No. The store can't be changed after the banner is created. Create a new banner for the other store and deactivate or delete the old one.

**Where does a banner link open?**
In a new browser tab, so your storefront stays open in the original tab.

**Can I show a banner overnight, for example 10:00 PM to 2:00 AM?**
A time window can't run past midnight. Use the whole day instead, or end the window at 11:59 PM.

**Do banners use my time zone or the shopper's?**
Your store's time zone. A banner scheduled for 4:00 PM appears at 4:00 PM store time for every shopper.

**How many banners can I have?**
You can create several banners per store and arrange them between your product category rows. Keep the landing page focused: two or three banners at a time is usually enough.
