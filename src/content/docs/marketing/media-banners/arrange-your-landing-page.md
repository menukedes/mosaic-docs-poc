Your landing page is made up of product category rows and media banners. You decide the order they appear in and which ones are shown, from a single reorder panel in the Admin Portal.

## Before you start

- Make sure the store you want to arrange is the one selected in the Admin Portal. Each store has its own landing page layout.
- Create any new banners first. See [Create a media banner](../create-a-media-banner/).

## Steps

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Marketing** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Reorder Widgets** button at the top right. The **Reorder Landing Page Widgets** panel opens, listing your category rows and media banners in their current order.
3. To move an item, drag it up or down the list.
4. To show or hide an item on the landing page, tick or untick the box next to it.
5. Save your changes.

On the right, the landing page shows Flower, then the Weekend special banner, then Edibles. The unticked banner doesn't appear.](../../../../assets/illustrations/media-banners-reorder.svg)

## What to expect

- The landing page shows items from top to bottom in the order of the list.
- Unticked items don't appear on the landing page. Unticking a media banner here is the same as deactivating it from the **Media Banners** list.
- A ticked media banner with a schedule still appears only during its scheduled dates, days and times.
- Refresh the storefront to see your changes.

## Examples

**Put a deal at the top.** Example Dispensary is running a weekend deal. The manager drags the "Weekend special" banner to the top of the list so it's the first thing shoppers see, then drags it back below the "Flower" row on Monday.

**Prepare a banner in advance.** The manager creates a "Holiday hours" banner in early December and leaves it unticked. On December 20 they tick it, and it appears on the landing page. (Alternatively, they could [schedule it](../schedule-a-media-banner/) and let it switch on by itself.)
