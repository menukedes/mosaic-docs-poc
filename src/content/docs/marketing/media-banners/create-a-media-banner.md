Create a media banner to promote a deal, product or announcement on your storefront landing page. It takes a few minutes once your images or video are ready.

## Before you start

- **Access**: you need access to **Marketing** in the Admin Portal.
- **Choose the store.** Each banner belongs to one store, and you can't move it to another store later. To show the same banner at several stores, create one banner for each store.
- **Prepare your images or video.**
  - Use wide, landscape images and videos made for a banner. Assets that aren't tall enough can have their sides cropped.
  - Check the design works at both phone and desktop widths. Keep important text and logos away from the edges.
  - Name your files using only letters, numbers and dashes, for example `weekend-special-1.jpg`. Avoid apostrophes and other special characters.
- **Decide where each banner should link to (optional).** Open the page on your storefront you want shoppers to land on, such as a category or product page, and copy its full web address from your browser's address bar.

## Steps

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Marketing** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Media Banners** tab.
3. Select **+ Create new**. A panel opens on the right.
4. In **Location**, select the store this banner is for.
5. In **Title**, enter a name that helps you recognize the banner later, for example "Weekend special – October".
6. Choose the banner type:
   - **Image carousel** for one image or several rotating images.
   - **Video** for a video.
7. Upload your image or images, or your video.
8. *(Optional)* Add a link for each image or video. Paste the full web address you copied earlier. Leave it empty if the banner shouldn't be clickable.
9. *(Image carousels with more than one image)* In **Carousel duration**, set how long each image stays on screen before the next one appears.
10. Choose when the banner appears:
    - **Activate Immediately** to show it as soon as you save.
    - **Schedule Activation** to choose dates, days and times. See [Schedule a media banner](../schedule-a-media-banner/).
11. Select **Save & Close**.

:::tip
Give banners clear titles that include the store and month, such as "Downtown – Weekend special – Oct". The title is how you find the banner in the list and when arranging your landing page.
:::

## What to expect

- The new banner appears in the **Media Banners** list with its status.
- If its status shows **Inactive**, open the **⋯** menu next to it and select **Activate**. See [Manage media banners](../manage-media-banners/).
- The banner is added to your landing page layout. To move it up or down, or check it's switched on, see [Arrange your landing page](../arrange-your-landing-page/).
- Shoppers see the banner on your storefront landing page while it's active and within its schedule. Refresh the storefront to see your changes.
- Selecting a banner with a link opens the linked page in a new browser tab. On in-store kiosks, banners aren't clickable.

## Examples

**A weekend deal at one store.** Example Dispensary wants a "20% off pre-rolls this weekend" banner at its Downtown store. The manager creates an **Image carousel** banner with one image, links it to the pre-rolls category page, and schedules it for Friday to Sunday.

**Three featured brands.** For a brand spotlight, Example Dispensary creates an **Image carousel** with three images, one per brand, each linking to that brand's products. They set **Carousel duration** so each image stays long enough to read.

**A product launch video.** For a new product line, Example Dispensary uploads a short **Video** banner and links it to the new products. Because they have two stores, they create the banner twice, once for each store.
