Create a message to reach your registered shoppers by email, push notification or both. Allow 10 to 15 minutes for your first message.

## Before you start

- **Decide your audience.** All registered shoppers, or specific [segments](../../segments/) and loyalty tiers.
- **Prepare your content.** For email: a title, the body text and any images. For push: a short subject and body of up to 50 characters each.
- **Keep emails light.** Use compressed images. Very large emails with many heavy images can fail to send.
- **Decide when to send.** Now, or at a scheduled date and time in your store's time zone.

## Steps

### Set up the message

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Marketing** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Messaging** tab, then select **Create New**. A panel opens on the right.
3. In **Location**, select the store sending the message.
4. Tick the channels to use: **Email**, **Push Notification**, or both.

### Choose recipients

5. Under **Recipients**, choose all registered shoppers, or choose one or more segments.
6. *(Optional)* Choose loyalty tiers to narrow the audience further. A note under the selection confirms who will receive the message.

:::caution
Recipients start empty every time. Choose them on purpose, including when you edit a draft or clone an older message.
:::

### Write the email

7. In **Stored Email Template**, choose a template. Select **Preview Selected Email Template** to see how it looks.
8. Open **Title & Subject** and enter:
   - The **title**, shown as the heading inside the email.
   - The **subject**, shown in the shopper's inbox.
9. Open **Body Content** and write your message. You can add images, links, tables, colors and font styles.

### Write the push notification

10. Enter a **Subject** and **Body**, up to 50 characters each. Some phones show only the first 37 or so characters of the subject, so put the key words first.
11. In **Destination URL**, choose the storefront page that opens when a shopper taps the notification.

### Preview, schedule and send

12. Open **Admin Email Addresses**. Your own email is included. Add any teammates who should check the email, then select **Send Admin Preview Email**. Check the preview in your inbox, on both a computer and a phone if you can.
13. *(Optional)* To send later, tick the scheduling option and choose a date and time in your store's time zone.
14. Check the recipient breakdown. For email it shows **Valid**, **Duplicates**, **Blacklisted**, **Unsubscribed** and **Total**. For push it shows **Opted In**, **Opted Out** and **Total**.
15. Tick the confirmation box that says the campaign is ready to be sent out to the recipients.
16. Select the send button to send now, or the schedule button if you chose a date and time.

You can select **Save as Draft** at any point after entering a title and come back later.

## What to expect

- The message appears in the **Messaging** list with the status **Scheduled** or **In Progress**, then **Completed** once sent.
- Only **Valid** email recipients receive the email. Duplicates are sent once, and shoppers who unsubscribed or whose address can't receive email are skipped automatically.
- Push notifications reach only the **Opted In** shoppers.
- Large audiences are sent in batches. Select **Refresh** on the list to update the status.
- If a message fails, you'll see the reason in the list, and your store's admins receive an email about it the next day.

## Examples

**Weekend deal announcement.** Example Dispensary sends an email and push to all registered shoppers on Friday at 10:00 AM: a "20% off pre-rolls this weekend" email with a product photo, and a push saying "Weekend deal: 20% off pre-rolls" that opens the storefront.

**Welcome series for new shoppers.** Every Monday, the manager sends an email to the **New Users** segment introducing the loyalty program and the store's best sellers.

**VIP early access.** For a new product drop, Example Dispensary emails the **OGs** segment in its top loyalty tier the evening before the public announcement.
