## My offer isn't showing on the storefront

Check these in order:

1. **Has it started?** Offer times are in UTC. A 9:00 AM UTC start is early morning in US time zones.
2. **Has it ended?** Offers end at 11:59 PM UTC on the end date, which is the afternoon or evening in US time zones.
3. **Is the shopper in the audience?** Guests only see offers for **All Users**. Registered-only offers need the shopper to sign in, and tier offers need the right loyalty tier.
4. **Is it the right store?** Check the offer covers the store the shopper is ordering from.
5. **Refresh the storefront.** Select **Update Storefront** on the **Offers** page and confirm.

## The discount shows in the cart but not as a strikethrough price

- Select **Update Storefront** on the **Offers** page, especially just after an offer starts.
- Product offers with a **Minimum Order Quantity** above 1 only show the discounted price once the minimum is in the cart.
- Order Total, Birthday, Sign-up and Date and Time offers never show as strikethrough prices; they apply to the whole cart.

## New products aren't getting my category offer

Products added to a category after the offer started may not pick it up straight away. Select **Update Storefront**.

## I can't find a product when creating a Product offer

Search for part of the product name instead of the full name, or search **By Category**.

## I can't change my offer

Active offers only allow changes to the name and description, and inactive offers can't be changed or switched back on. Create a new offer with the settings you need.

## I can't switch off an active offer

Contact the Mosaic support team, who can switch it off for you.

## A shopper's offers disappeared when they entered a promo code

If a promo code doesn't work, ask the shopper to re-open **Apply Offers & Rewards** and apply their offer again.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can two offers apply to the same product?**
No. The offer with the biggest saving applies.

**Can shoppers use an offer and a loyalty reward together?**
Yes.

**Can I make an offer for guests?**
Yes. Choose **All Users** for a Category, Product or Order Total offer.

**Do offers work in my store, at the counter?**
Mosaic offers apply to online orders. POS offers advertise discounts your POS applies in store.

**How many people can I add to an Individual Date and Time offer?**
Up to 100 email addresses.
