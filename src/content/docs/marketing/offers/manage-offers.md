After you create an offer, Mosaic switches it on and off for you. This page explains the timing, what you can still change, and how to update the storefront.

## When offers start and end

- An offer **switches on** at the start date and time you set.
- It **switches off** at 11:59 PM UTC on its end date.
- All offer times are in **UTC**, not your store's time zone.

| Your store is on | UTC is | 11:59 PM UTC is |
|---|---|---|
| US Eastern, summer | 4 hours ahead | 7:59 PM Eastern |
| US Eastern, winter | 5 hours ahead | 6:59 PM Eastern |
| US Pacific, summer | 7 hours ahead | 4:59 PM Pacific |
| US Pacific, winter | 8 hours ahead | 3:59 PM Pacific |

:::caution
Because offers end at 11:59 PM UTC, an offer ends in the afternoon or evening of its end date in US time zones. To run an offer through the whole of Sunday in the US, set the end date to Monday.
:::

:::tip
Always set an end date, even for long-running offers. You can create a new offer when it ends.
:::

## Find your offers

1. In the Admin Portal, select **Marketing** from the left menu.
2. Select the **Offers** tab.

The list shows each offer and its status.

## Change an offer

| Offer status | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Not started yet** | Everything. |
| **Active** | The name and description only. To change products, categories, discount or dates, create a new offer and [end the old one early](#end-an-offer-early). |
| **Inactive** | Nothing. An inactive offer can't be switched back on. Create a new offer instead. |

:::caution
Editing an active offer can send the activation email to your audience again. Avoid unnecessary edits to live offers.
:::

## End an offer early

To end an active offer before its end date, contact the Mosaic support team. Once an offer has ended it can't be switched back on; to run it again, create a new one.

## Update the storefront

Offer changes reach the storefront automatically on a regular schedule. To push them through straight away, for example right after an offer starts or after you add new products to a category, select **Update Storefront** on the **Offers** page and confirm.
